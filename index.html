Speed is turned into acceleration, then force = mass × acceleration + aerodynamic drag + rolling resistance + hills. Wheel power is force × speed, and crank power adds drivetrain loss and the energy spent spinning up the engine. Density altitude is filled in from your log's pressure and temperature (or from GPS altitude) and sets the air density; you can overwrite it. With GPS only there is no RPM, so power is plotted against speed. Enter RPM per mph for the gear you used to get RPM and torque.
